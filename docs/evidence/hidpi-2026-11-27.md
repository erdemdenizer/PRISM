# High-DPI rendering at 150 % and 200 % (Windows), 2026-11-27

The redesign was checked at 100 % and 125 % only ([RESPONSIVE_MATRIX_TR.md](../design/RESPONSIVE_MATRIX_TR.md)).
This records a check at 150 % and 200 %, made without changing the machine's display scale.

## Method

- Commit `26c9ee5` plus documentation-only changes, Windows 11 build 26300, Temurin 25, one 96 DPI display.
- PRISM ran through `mvnw javafx:run` with `JDK_JAVA_OPTIONS=-Dglass.win.uiScale=150%`, then `200%`. This makes
  JavaFX render at that scale, as it does on a display Windows scales.
- Only PRISM's own window was captured: its top-level window was found by process, and `PrintWindow` with
  `PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT` copied it. The desktop was never captured.

## Result

| Scale | Window | Capture |
|---|---|---|
| 150 % | 1666 × 1089 | [hidpi-150.png](hidpi-2026-11-27/hidpi-150.png) |
| 200 % | 2216 × 1439 | [hidpi-200.png](hidpi-2026-11-27/hidpi-200.png) |

- At both scales, text is sharp, and every measure grows by the scale (navigation rail, page header, panel padding,
  readouts).
- The four history graphs are drawn at the scale: Canvas line widths, the grid and the area fill show no blur and no
  half-pixel seams.
- No text is clipped, and nothing overlaps. The Overview keeps its 2 × 2 layout.

## Not checked

- Only the Overview page. Reaching the other pages needed input sent to the desktop, which was not done.
- The system title bar. With a forced JavaFX scale, Windows still draws the caption at 96 DPI, as the captures show.
  On a display Windows scales to 150 % or 200 %, Windows scales it. The title bar's colours are covered by
  [TITLEBAR_VALIDATION_TR.md](../design/TITLEBAR_VALIDATION_TR.md).
- A real 150 % or 200 % display, a move between displays of different scales, and Linux or macOS scaling.
